# Report each Document selection diagnostic as its own fact

`DocumentSelectionLifecycle` and its three phase reporters each expose a `diagnostic` method taking an `ExtractionRunObservation`, and the three on the reporters have byte-identical bodies. They stop being identical rather than being collapsed into one. Each becomes a method named for the fact it carries: `missing_input(path)` and `discovery_failed(path, detail)` on the lifecycle, `discovery_failed(path, detail)` on `DocumentDiscoveryProgress`, and `declarations_unreadable(path, detail)` on both `EpubFilteringProgress` and `EpubDeduplicationProgress`, each supplying its own purpose. The loose parameter is deleted. Across all eight call sites the old methods were only ever passed three variants, and the two `UnreadableEpubMetadata` sites differed solely by a `purpose` constant the phase already knew — so the bodies were identical because they had been abstracted one notch above the facts they carry, and pushing them back down removes the duplication instead of sharing it.

This supersedes two paragraphs of ADR-0004. That ADR recorded the widened `diagnostic` parameter as a deliberately traded-away compile-time guarantee and named the honest fix as "a diagnostic-only constructor on the observation module rather than a restored parallel vocabulary"; the form here is a third thing, and it is stronger. A constructor still leaves a method that accepts any diagnostic, so it would block `Terminal(..)` while leaving a filtering call site free to report a deduplication purpose. Per-fact methods make both unrepresentable, because no caller states a purpose and no caller constructs an observation. ADR-0004 also recorded the lifecycle's ignorance of which diagnostic it emits — "its only job is where the fact is emitted" — and that ignorance is given up here. It was not paying rent: with eight call sites and three variants, where a fact is emitted and which fact it is are decided in the same place anyway, and the ignorance cost exactly the guarantee ADR-0004 then had to write a paragraph apologising for.

The silence gate becomes one crate-private free function in the module, `emit_when(observer, active, observation)`, used at ten of the twelve gated emission points — the initial observation of all three lifecycle phase methods, discovery's final observation, the three reporters' diagnostics, `document_discovered`, and both `record_check` bodies. The final observations of filtering and deduplication stay inline, because each is preceded by a completeness assertion that must share its guard: an inactive phase asserts nothing today, so routing the emission through the gate while leaving the assertion behind its own `if active` would test the same flag twice and separate the assertion from the emission it exists to justify. Discovery's final observation has no assertion and so has no such tie. A value owning the observer and the active flag was considered and rejected: the lifecycle emits its initial observation before the reporter exists and its final one after the reporter is dropped, so such a value could serve only six of the twelve points and would leave the gate written in two places, which is the whole thing it exists to prevent. The two ungated lifecycle diagnostics cannot reach the gate by accident, because `DocumentSelectionLifecycle` has no active flag to pass it.

One reporter parameterised over running and finished constructors, instantiated three times, was considered and rejected. The three reporters are not three copies of one shape: `DocumentDiscoveryProgress` carries a scope and a growing `discovered` count with no denominator, no `checked`, no `record_check` and no completeness assertion, while filtering and deduplication carry `checked` against a known `total` and assert that every candidate was accounted for. Fitting all three behind one reporter means either an optional total whose absence is legal for exactly one phase — the illegal-state-in-the-type shape ADR-0007 exists to remove — or a tally trait with an associated outcome type, which widens the call-site type from `&mut EpubFilteringProgress<'_>` to a generic and adds a trait to read before either bounded phase can be read. Its remaining advantage, that a fourth phase would cost only an instantiation, is speculative: nothing in `docs/`, `plans/`, `CONTEXT.md` or `README.md` plans one.

Collapsing the three lifecycle phase methods was considered and rejected. They share a real skeleton and a real invariant — an active phase emits exactly one initial and exactly one final observation, currently stated three times — and the borrow checker permits a helper taking make-reporter, initial, finish and body closures if `finish` consumes the reporter. The result is a shallow abstraction: understanding one phase would mean reading the helper and all four of its closures where today it means reading one method. The three methods stay three.

A shared bounded-phase counter owning the `checked < total` guard and the `checked == total` assertion was considered and rejected. Each of those guards has exactly one call site per phase and two in total, so the helper would centralise invariants that are not duplicated enough to be at risk, and it costs more lines than the four sites it absorbs. Filtering and deduplication keep their own guards, and both stay hard `assert!` rather than `debug_assert!`: they fire only on a bug in this module, and a progress line reading "checked 4 of 7, finished" is worse than a panic.

The architecture review that prompted this change proposed the rejected shape and predicted that roughly two thirds of the 307-line module would go. It will not. `progress.rs` ends up about the same size or slightly larger, because five typed methods cost more lines than four loose ones and the call sites are what get shorter. That is recorded here so a later reader comparing the review to the result does not conclude the change was abandoned half-done. The gain is structural and countable in a different unit: one silence gate instead of twelve, the wrong observation and the wrong purpose both made compile errors, and eight call sites that no longer construct observations.

Two names change as a consequence rather than as a decision of their own. `UnreadableEpubMetadata` becomes `UnreadableEpubDeclarations` and `EpubMetadataPurpose` becomes `EpubDeclarationPurpose`, because `CONTEXT.md` defines **EPUB declarations** and lists "EPUB metadata" among the synonyms to avoid, and these new per-fact methods would otherwise be named in the disfavoured vocabulary. This is the same fix ADR-0004 recorded for the discovery phase when it took the glossary's Document discovery vocabulary over the scan synonym, and it is recorded the same way — inside the ADR of the change that carries it, not as an ADR of its own. The enum survives the rename because Extraction run presentation renders the two purposes differently; what it loses is the ability for a caller to pick the wrong one.

`CONTEXT.md` gains one sentence with this ADR rather than with the code, and that is not the contradiction of ADR-0008 it may look like. ADR-0008 deferred its glossary entry because it was defining a term for a thing the crate did not yet have. The sentence added here names a fact that is already true of the existing code — Document discovery has no denominator until it has finished finding, while filtering and deduplication run against a known candidate set every member of which must be accounted for — which is precisely why two phases assert completeness and the third cannot. No new term is added: a separate "Document selection phase" entry would exist only to prop up another entry.

The change lands in three further commits. First the rename, alone, touching twenty-one occurrences across five source files; it edits eight assertions in `document_selection/tests.rs`, and isolating it keeps an edited test readable as a rename rather than as evidence of changed behaviour. Then the per-fact methods. Then the silence gate, whose diff is uniformly one collapsed conditional per site. The emitted observation stream is byte-identical throughout, so every existing ordering, monotonicity and silence test passes unmodified after the rename commit, and a test that needs editing is to be read as evidence the refactor changed behaviour rather than as a test to fix. No tests are added and `progress.rs` gains no test module: the purpose mismatch becomes a compile error and needs none, all three inactive-phase silence paths are already pinned, and the module is entirely `pub(super)` and correctly exercised through `select_documents`, so unit tests there would pin implementation.
